Synthesis

These capabilities do not exist independently. Their value comes from the connections between them.

What each part does

  • Strategy shapes direction.
  • Finance measures economics.
  • Operations executes.
  • Quality improves the system.
  • Data makes performance visible.
  • Governance creates accountability.
  • Decision intelligence connects information to action.
  • Digital systems make the model scalable.

What they add up to

Business systems thinking means treating those as one system rather than eight functions: a capital allocation decision is also a cost-structure decision, a capacity decision and a governance decision.

The three syntheses on the map — decision intelligence, digital transformation, and business systems thinking above them — are not extra skills. They are what the connections produce.
