Undergraduate thesis

Performance Assessment of the Self-Service Queuing System — Pertamina Gas Station, Dago Bandung

It measured whether a self-service refuelling system solved the queue it was installed to solve. The bottleneck turned out to sit in the first phase of the process, and the system still performed better overall than the conventional one. An early example of what still interests me: whether a change to how a business operates does what it was meant to do.
