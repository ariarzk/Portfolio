Practice

Three lines of work, held together rather than run in sequence — the plant, the capital that funds it, and the controls that keep both honest.

Operating model

Manufacturing strategy and operating model: shareholder objectives translated into systems, KPIs and governance frameworks. Structural cost efficiency through automation and workforce redesign, with accountability for operating performance and cost structure.

Capital allocation

A company-wide investment policy governing allocation, risk thresholds and return discipline — then the major capital investments evaluated, financed and executed under it, from manufacturing capacity to out-of-city sales expansion.

Governance and control

Decision-making and risk governance across production, quality and capital expenditure, with independent QA embedded. Budgeting, forecasting, working capital and long-term planning behind it, and bank financing re-established through rebuilt lender relationships.
