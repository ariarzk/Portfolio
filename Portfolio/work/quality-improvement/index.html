BT-03/Operations/Case study

Quality Improvement Project

A quality problem taken from customer complaint to controlled process — without missing sales.

Role

VP Manufacture

Domain

Quality · Process engineering

Period

2024 — 2026

Partner

LAPI ITB

01

Situation

On taking responsibility for manufacturing, the complaint register was climbing steeply. The second half of 2024 ran at roughly seven times the rate of the first — a deterioration in months, not a standing condition.

Complaints are escaped defects: what reached a customer and came back. They are the last and most expensive place a quality problem shows up, and they were the only signal available: no in-process or laboratory data existed to explain why.

02

Challenge

The cause was not visible from inside the plant’s own records. It sat in the water treatment process — filtration, disinfection, piping — where the operating practice was long established, assumed correct and never measured.

Two constraints shaped what followed. The plant did not have the analytical capability to settle the question itself, and the work would need production stopped in a business that still had to fill its orders.

03

Approach

Rather than guess, I commissioned a process and facilities assessment, as business sponsor, from LAPI ITB, the engineering consultancy of Institut Teknologi Bandung, and set its scope, the investment priorities and the success criteria.

Five engineering disciplines worked the plant at once — environmental, chemical, microbiology, mechanical, and electrical control and instrumentation. Samples were taken going in and going out of every process unit rather than at the end of the line, raw water and product were tested against the full regulatory parameter set, and microbial populations were characterised in the laboratory.

The findings then went through the company’s own investment route — a business requirement document and an investment case under the framework in BT-01 — so the fix was funded as capital work with a scope, a cost and a stated benefit, rather than absorbed into maintenance.

04

Execution

The action list followed the findings rather than the symptoms. Cartridge washing stopped and replacement took its place; filtration was tightened, with the pumping to carry it. Disinfection capacity was restored to the maximum operating flow and given online measurement, so it could be checked rather than assumed. Process piping was replaced in stainless steel and the tanks cleaned; a further treatment stage was added downstream. The filling area was closed up — hygienic housekeeping under standard procedure, positive pressure, filtered air.

Sequencing was the hard part. Each of those steps needs the line down, and the plant was still filling orders, so the work was staged around production.

05

Result

The register turned in the last quarter of 2025, once the facility and process work was in, and 2026 has held. On the same January to July window either side, the complaint rate fell from 35.0 to 13.0 PPM and complaint events from 345 to 65. The most recent months run between roughly one and five PPM, against 2025 months that reached the fifties.

  • Complaint rate down 63% and complaint events down 81%, like-for-like.
  • Cost of poor quality down from 2.46% of revenue to 0.98%, like-for-like — a peak month of 3.47% in 2025 against a best month of 0.77% in 2026.
  • Failure cost moved inside the plant: external failure fell 91% as a share of revenue, from two-fifths of the quality bill to under a tenth.
  • Quality is now a standing management view rather than a monthly count — the register runs as a module of the Terminal, with defect families, shelf-age patterns and recurrence after corrective action.
  • Sales fulfilment maintained through every stoppage the rebuild required.
  • One spike remains, in February 2026, from a single large event; it is in the chart rather than smoothed out.

The view

The plant was cleaning the thing that was contaminating it.

No one was careless. A reasonable maintenance practice, never measured, had become the source of the problem it was meant to prevent, and the disinfection stage was not reducing anything. Neither shows up without measuring in and out of every unit.
